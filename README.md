# Guyana-Morning-Paper
A compilation of all of the day's news scraped from local news sources and compiled by Claude AI everyday at 530am. 
It is aimed to be a newspaper that allows the reader to access all the relevant news and have all the sources at their fingertips allowing them to form their own opinions free from biases. While the summaries are prepared by AI, the sources that reported on that headline are one click away so that each reader can read in full detail - should they say desire.
